Flexible remote opportunities for Singapore-based applicants

  • Remote-first
  • Flexible schedules
  • Third-party hiring

Scoutnect is a platform where individuals in Singapore can submit their profile to be considered for remote or flexible roles that may be offered by third-party hiring companies.

Common role categories include AI data evaluation, customer support, administrative support, content support, e-commerce support, and other online-based work.

Role availability, responsibilities, pay structure, and hiring criteria vary depending on the hiring company and may not be available for every applicant.

Compensation

How pay works

Most roles may be compensated based on hourly rates, per task, per shift, or per project, depending on the hiring company.

When pay details are provided

Specific pay rates, payment terms, and schedules are typically confirmed directly by the hiring company during their hiring process.

Variable pay factors

Actual pay depends on the company, role scope, working hours, experience, and other criteria.

Important note

Any pay examples or ranges shown are general in nature and may not apply to all opportunities.

Eligibility

Who can take part

Before applying, please ensure you meet the general requirements below. Individual roles may have additional criteria set by hiring companies.

Eligibility
  • Meet the minimum working age under Singapore regulations
  • Have the appropriate eligibility to work where required by the hiring company
Communication
Able to communicate in the required language for the role. Comfortable using email, chat, or online tools.
Remote setup
Access to a suitable device and stable internet connection. Able to complete tasks in a consistent environment.
Accurate information
Provide accurate contact and background details. Be prepared for standard verification steps if required.
